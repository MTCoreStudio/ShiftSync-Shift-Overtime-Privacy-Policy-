# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

**ShiftSync: Shift & Overtime** ("ShiftSync", "we", "the app") is a productivity app developed
by **MT Core Studio** (contact: `mtcorestudio@gmail.com`). This policy explains what data the app
collects, how it is used, and your choices. By using the app you agree to this policy.

> The canonical, live copy of this policy is hosted at:
> `https://github.com/MTCoreStudio/ShiftSync-Shift-Overtime-Privacy-Policy-`

## 1. Data you create

Your shifts, settings and schedule are stored **locally on your device** using on-device
storage (AsyncStorage). **We do not operate servers, and we do not collect, transmit, or
store your schedule on our systems.** You can delete everything at any time from
**Settings → Clear local data**.

## 2. Camera & photo library

The **camera** and **photo library** are used only to capture and open shift rosters so the
on-device OCR feature can auto-fill your schedule. Recognised text is processed **entirely on
your device** using Google ML Kit OCR. Photos you scan are **never uploaded** anywhere, and
we cannot access them.

## 3. Advertising

ShiftSync is free and funded by advertising. We use **Google AdMob** as the primary ad
network and may use **Start.io** as a fallback.

- Before we serve ads, the app shows a **consent screen** powered by Google's
  **User Messaging Platform (UMP)** so that advertising complies with the **GDPR / EU User
  Consent Policy, UK data-protection law, and US state privacy laws** (including regulated US
  states).
- Where consent is required but not yet obtained, **no personalised ads** are shown.
- Ad networks may collect and process data such as:
  - Device identifiers (e.g. the Android Advertising ID)
  - Approximate location
  - App usage and interaction data
  - Technical device information

This data is used to deliver and measure advertising. It is subject to the ad networks' own
privacy policies:
- Google AdMob: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- Google UMP: <https://support.google.com/admob/answer/10113027>
- Start.io: <https://www.start.io/privacy>

## 4. Permissions

- **Camera** — to photograph shift rosters for on-device OCR.
- **Photo library (system picker)** — opening existing roster images from your gallery uses
  the **Android system photo picker** (and the iOS system picker), which works without any
  broad photo/video storage permission. Selected images are only used for on-device OCR and
  are never uploaded.
- **Internet** — required to load and display ads (after consent). No personal data from
  your schedules is transmitted.
- **Advertising ID** — used by ad networks for advertising and measurement, subject to your
  UMP consent choices and your device's ad personalisation settings.

## 5. Data safety summary (as listed on the Play Store)

- **Data collected:** None collected by the developer. The app does not collect or share
  your schedule data.
- **Data encrypted in transit:** Yes (HTTPS only).
- **Data deletion:** Your schedule can be deleted on-device from Settings → Clear local data.

## 6. Your choices

- **Ad personalisation:** You can opt out of personalised advertising in your device's
  **Google → Ads** settings at any time.
- **Consent & privacy options:** At any time you can review or update your consent choices
  (a "Privacy Options" form is provided by UMP where applicable).
- **Delete data:** Remove all locally stored schedule data from **Settings → Clear local data**.

## 7. Children

The app is intended for general audiences (productivity/tools). It does not knowingly
collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information
through an ad, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available at the
privacy-policy repository linked above, and the in-app "Settings → About" reflects the terms
under which the app operates.
